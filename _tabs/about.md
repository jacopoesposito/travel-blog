# A journal of places and moments

I'm Jacopo. I travel to discover new places, experience different cultures, and observe the details that make each journey unique.

This is a personal archive of my travels — stories, photographs, and notes collected along the way. A place to remember where I've been, what I've seen, and what stayed with me.

No fixed agenda, just curiosity and an open road.
